Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Who we are

VersoCreatives is a small design studio based in the Netherlands. We sell printable coloring books, advent coloring calendars and planners through our Etsy shop, and some coloring books as paperbacks on Amazon. We are the data controller for the processing described below.

This website

This website only shows our products and links to them. It has no contact forms, no accounts, no newsletter, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising trackers. We do not collect any personal data through it.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address in its server logs to deliver and protect the website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Purchases on Etsy and Amazon

Orders are placed and paid on Etsy or Amazon, under their own privacy policies. For Etsy orders we receive only the information Etsy shares with sellers (such as your name, username and order details) and use it only to handle your order, answer your messages and meet our legal and tax obligations. Digital files are delivered by Etsy. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else. Order records are kept for as long as Dutch tax law requires (usually 7 years).

Our Pinterest tool

We use a small internal tool that connects to the Pinterest API only for our own Pinterest business account. It creates Pins for our own products on our own boards, and every Pin is approved by us before it is published. The tool does not access, collect or store any data about other Pinterest users. Access tokens are stored encrypted on our own computer and are never shared.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive a copy of it. To exercise these rights, send us a message through our Etsy shop. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
